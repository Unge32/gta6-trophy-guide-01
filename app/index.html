    The road to platinum

    Five stages, in the order to play them. Story first — most story trophies pop on their own — then side missions, then activities, then collectibles, then the money and mayhem cleanup. Every stage shows its own progress; the platinum pops when all 54 others are done.

      Missable trophies — do these when the chance comes

      Nine trophies are flagged missable from their official descriptions: one-shot moments, partner moments, or choices that may not come back. These flags are judged from the descriptions alone and are not yet confirmed in-game — GTA games usually let you replay missions, so treat each flag as “do it while the chance is in front of you”, not as a proven point of no return. Before the final mission, work through this list.

        Sources & honesty

        Every trophy's name, description and grade are the official PlayStation 5 list as published, taken from the two public listings that agreed on all 55 on 18 November 2026:

        • Exophase — Grand Theft Auto VI trophies: the official names, descriptions and grades. exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies
        • PSNProfiles — Grand Theft Auto VI trophies: the counts PlayStation tracks for each trophy (250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone races, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, and the rest). psnprofiles.com/trophies/43531-grand-theft-auto-vi

        Every guide in this app is written in our own words from those official descriptions and counts. We have not played the game, and no guide text is copied from any other guide, wiki, video or forum — a source is cited, never copied. Where a step or a place is not pinned down by the official description, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing a location. As players confirm the real routes, the guides should be updated and the missable flags re-checked.

        No online trophies: the official list has no online-only trophy, so there is no online filter to fill. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork, box art or game screenshot is used anywhere in this app; the look is our own.
